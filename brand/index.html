Two T's.
One block at a time.

The mark is a 6 × 3 grid of square tiles. Green tiles spell a T and its mirror, TT. Everything else is the field around them. No wordmark, ever: the mark is the name.

Colour

Neon Green and Dark Blue are the anchors. The spectrum exists to be used loudly: pick one or two per piece, and let the rest breathe.

  • Bright colours (Green, Lagoon, Sun, Flame, Hibiscus) go on Dark Blue or Ink. Contrast is 3.2–13:1.
  • On Mist, use Dark Blue (10.5:1) or Violet (4.6:1) for text and mark. Never neon on light.
  • Body text: Mist on Ink, or Dark Blue on Mist. Hibiscus and Flame are for large shapes, not small text.

Primary mark

Left: the primary mark (mark.svg). Right: the prism mark (mark-prism.svg), where each block of the field takes its own spectrum colour. Use the prism where you want energy (social, stickers, merch), the primary everywhere else.

Construction & space

One tile = one unit. The mark is 6 × 3 units. Keep clear space of one tile on every side; minimum width is 24 px (12 px per T). Below that, drop the field and use the T's alone (mark-mono.svg).

Type

Aa Bb Tt 123

Dela Gothic One for headlines: one heavy weight, flat and blocky like the tiles. Keep it short and large, sentence case. Martian Mono (300–800) for everything else: body, labels, code, hex values. The mono grid echoes the tile grid. The name appears as text, never as part of the logo.

UI preview

The rules from ui-ux.md applied to a page. Square corners, flat colour, one spectrum colour as the highlight, one primary action. Buttons invert on hover; try them.

Don't

  • Don't add a wordmark, tagline or outline to the mark.
  • Don't rotate, skew or stretch it. The only mirror is the one already in it.
  • Don't use more than three colours in one mark (prism is the ceiling).
  • Don't put neon colours on light backgrounds.
